About

Security work grounded in real infrastructure

My path into security started on the operations side — administering Active Directory, hardening endpoints, and learning how enterprise and industrial networks actually behave when something goes wrong. That internship at Hass Petroleum Kenya put me directly in front of a live IT/OT environment, where I designed and shipped a full monitoring and alerting pipeline that still runs today.

Since then I've concentrated on the defensive engineering side: building detection and alerting workflows that reduce noise, hardening Microsoft 365 and email infrastructure against the phishing and spoofing attacks I kept seeing in the wild, and translating frameworks like NIST and MITRE ATT&CK into controls that hold up in production.

I'm currently completing a BSc in Information Security and Forensics at KCA University, and building my digital forensics practice through hands-on lab work — disk, memory, and network artifacts — documented publicly. I'm looking for an entry-level or internship role where I can keep building detections, respond to real incidents, and grow into a full-spectrum defensive analyst.

Built a full-stack IT monitoring and alerting pipeline at Hass Petroleum using Zabbix (open-source). Integrated real-time notifications via Telegram and Microsoft 365 email. Configured SNMP monitoring for legacy network switches, deployed Snort IDS log ingestion, and automated daily HTML/PDF uptime reports delivered to Telegram.
